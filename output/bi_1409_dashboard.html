Methodology: every rate source (DBS Price list 2026, Ship Cost Matrix, Q3 AUGUST, MNT UK price list, Canot rate card, BayWa IT manual override) gives one flat price per shipment/order, not per line. Lines are grouped by Shipment Number (or SE Order# when there is none yet), priced once for the group's total whole-number pallets, then allocated pro-rata by pallet share. Rows with no Cost (USD) are flagged in the source report's Note column for a warehouse-contact check — mostly destinations outside DBS/matrix coverage or a BayWa IT shipment whose total pallets isn't one of the four rates on file.
